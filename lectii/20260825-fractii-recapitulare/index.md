$$
\prod_{1}^{49}\left(1 - \frac{1}{n+1}\right) = \prod_{1}^{49} \frac{n}{n+1}
$$

$$ 
\prod_{n=1}^{49} \frac{n}{n+1} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} \dots \frac{48}{49} \cdot \frac{49}{50} = \frac{1}{50}
$$
